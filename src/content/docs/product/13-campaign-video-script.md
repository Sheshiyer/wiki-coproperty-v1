## Co.Property 90-Second Conversion Video

---

## VIDEO STRATEGY

**Duration:** 90 seconds (optimal for platform algorithms + attention span)  
**Format:** Founder-led narrative with B-roll, data visualizations, testimonial clips  
**Tone:** Data-driven authority with empathetic understanding (Forbes documentary style)  
**Goal:** Drive consultation bookings by proving mechanism superiority

**Conversion Framework:**
1. Hook (0-5s): Problem statement creating immediate recognition
2. Problem & Hero (5-20s): Empathic setup + founder authority
3. Mechanism Reveal (20-40s): AI pricing + professional management explanation
4. How It Works (40-55s): 5-phase system overview
5. Feature Highlights (55-70s): 3 value-packed proof points
6. Social Proof (70-80s): Quick testimonial + data
7. Offer & CTA (80-90s): Partnership tiers + consultation call

## PRODUCTION SPECIFICATIONS

### Technical Requirements

**Video Format:**
- Resolution: 1920×1080 (Full HD minimum)
- Aspect Ratio: 16:9 (landscape for Kickstarter/Indiegogo/YouTube)
- Additional cuts: 1:1 (Instagram), 9:16 (TikTok/Reels/Stories)
- Frame Rate: 24fps (cinematic) or 30fps (web standard)
- Bitrate: 10-15 Mbps for high quality

**Audio:**
- Voiceover: Professional recording (home studio acceptable with good mic)
- Music: Royalty-free from Epidemic Sound or Artlist
- Sound Design: Subtle UI sounds for data visualizations
- Mix: -14 LUFS for web platform standards

**Editing Style:**
- Pacing: Quick cuts (2-4 seconds per shot) maintaining energy
- Transitions: Clean cuts, minimal fancy transitions
- Color Grade: Professional, slightly warm (not sterile)
- Subtitles: Burned-in captions (85% of social views are muted)

### Production Budget Estimates

**DIY Production (Founder-Led):**
- Equipment rental: $200-500
- Professional editor: $500-1,000
- Stock footage/music: $100-200
- **Total: $800-1,700**

**Professional Production:**
- Video production company: $3,000-8,000
- Includes: Crew, equipment, location, editing, graphics
- **Total: $3,000-8,000**

**Recommended Approach:**
Hybrid—founder records A-roll with professional videographer ($500-1,000), editor assembles with stock footage ($1,000-1,500), motion graphics designer handles animations ($800-1,200). **Total: $2,300-3,700** for high-quality result.

## PLATFORM-SPECIFIC VERSIONS

### YouTube / Campaign Page (90s Master)
- Full 90-second version as scripted
- Use for: Campaign hero section, YouTube pre-roll ads, organic social

### Instagram/Facebook Feed (60s Cut)
- Remove: Extended mechanism explanation (compress to 10s)
- Keep: Hook, problem, solution overview, social proof, CTA
- 1:1 aspect ratio, subtitles mandatory

### TikTok/Reels (30s Hook Version)
- Hook (5s) → Problem (10s) → Result (10s) → CTA (5s)
- Lead with: "Your neighbor earns 65K monthly. You earn 48K. Here's the gap."
- Fast-paced, high energy
- 9:16 vertical format

### LinkedIn (60s Professional)
- More business-focused tone
- Emphasize: ROI data, portfolio scalability, institutional-grade systems
- Less consumer emotion, more strategic benefit
- Include: "Featured in Bangkok Post, Thailand Property Report"

## TESTING & OPTIMIZATION

### A/B Test Variants

**Variant A: Problem-Led**
- Open with pain (2 AM messages, pricing stress)
- Longer problem agitation
- Shorter mechanism explanation
- Test with: Audience who experienced DIY frustration

**Variant B: Data-Led**
- Open with stat: "76.3% occupancy vs 52% market average"
- Lead with proof points
- Compress problem section
- Test with: Analytical investors (Singapore/Hong Kong demographic)

**Variant C: Founder Story**
- Open with founder direct-to-camera: "I watched investors lose 200K annually..."
- Personal narrative arc
- More emotional connection
- Test with: Audience valuing authenticity over data

### Performance Metrics

**Success Indicators:**
- View-through rate: >40% (viewers watching past 50% mark)
- Click-through rate: >5% (viewers clicking CTA)
- Consultation booking rate: >2% (viewers who book after watching)

**Optimization Triggers:**
- If VTR <25%: Hook isn't engaging, test new opening
- If CTR <3%: CTA isn't clear enough, strengthen end card
- If booking rate <1%: Value proposition unclear, adjust proof points
